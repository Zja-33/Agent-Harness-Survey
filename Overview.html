Beyond the Model:
The Power of the Agent Harness

Nowadays, the reliability of LLM agents in production is increasingly determined not by the underlying model, but by the agent harness that encapsulates it. As tasks grow longer, harness redesign alone achieves order-of-magnitude gains.

🔍

The Problem

Underlying models remain fixed, yet tasks are getting exponentially more complex, causing raw LLMs to fail in production.

⚒

The Solution

A systematic "Agent Harness" encapsulating the LLM, managing state, context, tools, and execution loops.

📈

The Impact

Order-of-magnitude reliability gains without retraining or updating the underlying foundation model.
